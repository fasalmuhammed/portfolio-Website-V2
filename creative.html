FAQ

Questions clients ask first.

How much does a website or app cost?

It depends on what you need. After a short call I send a fixed quote that lists every feature, so you know the full price before any work starts.

How long does a project take?

A business website usually takes one to three weeks. Custom web apps and AI systems take longer depending on features. You get a timeline with milestones in the quote.

Do you work with clients outside Kerala?

Yes. I'm based in Kozhikode and work remotely with businesses across India and abroad over video calls, WhatsApp and email.

Can you add AI to my existing website?

Yes. I can add a chatbot trained on your own content, automate enquiries, or plug machine learning into the systems you already use.

What happens after launch?

I handle deployment, train your team and stay available for fixes, updates and new features as your business grows.
